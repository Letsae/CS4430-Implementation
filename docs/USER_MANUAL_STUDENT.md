# User Manual — Student

## 1. Role and responsibilities

Students use the LMS to access their courses and assignments, submit work, and view marks and feedback when released.

## 2. Sign in

1. Start the LMS.
2. Enter your student username and password.
3. Confirm that the Student menu appears.

## 3. Main tasks

### View enrolled courses
1. Choose the course-list option.
2. Review the courses in which you are enrolled.
3. Select a course to view its available information.

If a course is missing, contact the Administrator or course staff to check your enrolment.

### View assignments
1. Select a course.
2. Open its assignment list.
3. Select an assignment to read the instructions and deadline.
4. Make sure you understand the submission requirements and due date.
5. If your lecturer has extended the deadline for you or for your class, the list shows the new deadline, for example `Deadline: 2035-06-05T12:00:00Z (extended from 2035-06-01T14:00:00Z)`, with the lecturer's reason on the next line. Lateness and penalties are measured against the new deadline. Check the list again if you were told about an extension, because you also receive a notification.

### Submit an assignment
1. Open the relevant assignment.
2. Choose the submission option.
3. Enter or provide your work using the method supported by the application.
4. Review your submission before confirming.
5. Confirm that the LMS displays a successful submission or updated status.

If resubmission is permitted, use the application's resubmission workflow and check the final status. Do not assume an earlier submission was replaced unless the LMS confirms it.

### View marks and feedback
1. Open the relevant course or assignment.
2. Choose the marks/results option.
3. View marks and lecturer feedback when they have been released.

Marks that are still awaiting moderation or release may not be visible.

## 4. Good practice

- Submit before the deadline.
- Check the submission confirmation/status.
- Keep a copy of your work.
- Protect your login credentials.
- Contact your lecturer promptly if you encounter a submission problem.

## 5. Troubleshooting

- **Cannot see a course:** check enrolment with the Administrator.
- **Cannot submit:** check the deadline, assignment status, and required fields.
- **No mark displayed:** the mark may not yet be moderated or released.
- **Submission status is uncertain:** contact your lecturer and provide the assignment name and time of attempted submission.
