# User Manual — Head of Department (HoD)

## 1. Role and responsibilities

The Head of Department (HoD) reviews marks submitted by lecturers for moderation, records moderation decisions, and follows the system's approval/release process. HoD access is limited to the relevant department and authorized moderation tasks.

## 2. Sign in

1. Start the LMS.
2. Enter your HoD username and password.
3. Confirm that the HoD menu appears.

## 3. Moderation workflow

### View items awaiting moderation
1. Open the moderation or pending-review option.
2. Select the relevant course/assignment if prompted.
3. Review the submissions and marks awaiting moderation.

### Review a mark
1. Open the relevant submission.
2. Review the lecturer's mark and feedback.
3. Compare the mark with the applicable departmental/course marking policy.
4. Decide whether to approve or adjust the mark, using only options available in the LMS.

### Approve or adjust
1. Select the approval or adjustment operation.
2. If adjusting, enter the authorized revised mark and a clear reason.
3. Confirm the action.
4. Verify the moderation result and audit information.

The system may record the original and revised values, the user, timestamp, and reason. Check the audit record after a moderation action where the application provides that view.

### Release marks
1. Confirm that the required moderation/approval steps are complete.
2. Use the release option only when authorized and available.
3. Verify the released status.
4. Confirm that students can view the marks only after release.

## 4. Good practice

- Apply the approved moderation policy consistently.
- Provide a reason for every mark adjustment.
- Do not approve your own work unless policy and system permissions explicitly allow it.
- Keep moderation decisions confidential.
- Use audit records to support accountability.

## 5. Troubleshooting

- **No pending items:** confirm the lecturer submitted marks for moderation and that you have the correct department/course access.
- **Cannot adjust or approve:** verify your role and the submission's current status.
- **Student cannot see a mark:** check whether the mark has been released.
- **Audit entry is unclear:** record the submission, time, action, and displayed audit details for the maintainer.
